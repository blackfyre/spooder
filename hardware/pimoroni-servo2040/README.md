# Pimoroni Servo 2040: reference files

The robot's board. Pimoroni publishes no 3D model; these are the two documents on the [product page](https://shop.pimoroni.com/products/servo-2040), copied unchanged on 2026-10-23. They are Pimoroni's, published without a stated licence.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `servo2040_schematic.pdf` | the schematic (3 pages) |
| `servo-2040-drawing.png` | the mechanical drawing, in millimetres |

Not copied: Pimoroni's [mounting bracket for Servo 2040](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:5929573) on Thingiverse.

## Dimensions (from the drawing)

- **Board:** 62.00 × 42.00 mm; 62 × 42 × 12 mm with connectors (product page).
- **Mounting holes:** four M2.5, 2.70 mm in from each edge (centres 56.6 × 36.6 mm apart).
- **Servo header:** 18 columns of signal, + and −, in three groups of six. The first column centre is 13.60 mm from the left edge, the groups 17.40 mm apart, and the signal row 3.90 mm from the top edge (rows 14.00 mm deep).
- **USB-C:** on the left edge, its centre 21.00 mm up from the bottom edge.
- **Sensor header** (6 sensors, the analog mux): on the right, its centre 3.90 mm in from the right edge and 16.30 mm up from the bottom edge.
- **Qw/ST connector:** on the bottom edge, its centre 9.20 mm from the left edge.
- **Lower header:** A1's centre is 34.81 mm from the left edge (5V, 3V3, SDA, SCL, INT, A0–A2, SWC, SWD, RST, BOOT).
